# Contact

Questions, problems, payments or feedback — write to:

<p class="contact-mail"><a href="mailto:sstp.pinger@gmail.com">sstp.pinger@gmail.com</a></p>

## Reporting a problem

To get an answer faster, include:

- your platform and version (Android, Windows or Linux; **Settings → This
  device** shows the app version),
- what you did and what happened,
- the log, if the app misbehaved — see
  [Sending a log](/docs/troubleshooting#sending-a-log).

Many questions are already answered in the [FAQ](/docs/faq) and
[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
