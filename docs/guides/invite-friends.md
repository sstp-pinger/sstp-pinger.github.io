# Invite friends

| | Reward |
|---|---|
| **Your friend** | **+7 days** at once, and the [free plan](./free-plan) |
| **You** | **+7 days** once your friend has used the app on 3 different days |
| **You, again** | **+7 / +14 / +28 days** when your friend first subscribes for 1 / 3 / 6+ months |

The rewards for using the app count up to four friends a month. Purchase
rewards have no limit.

## How to invite

1. In the app open **Subscription → Invite a friend** (or **Settings →
   Account → Invite a friend**).
2. Send your **link** (`https://t.me/sstp_shield_bot?start=ref_…`) or your
   **code** — both are yours for good. **Share via Telegram** picks a chat;
   **Copy link** works anywhere.
3. The card shows how many friends you invited, how many use the app, how many
   subscribed, and the days you earned.

**Send it privately** — to friends, in a private chat. Don't post it in public
channels or groups.

## What your friend does

1. Installs SSTP Shield and opens it once — the free trial starts by itself.
2. Either types your code in the app under **Have an invite code?** — no
   Telegram needed — or opens your link, joins our
   [channel](https://t.me/sstp_shield) in the bot and sends it their
   username (**Settings → Account → Username**).

The days show up in the app right away.

## Rules

- The friend must be **new**: an account from the free trial, started within
  the last week.
- An app account and a Telegram account each count **once, ever**. You can't
  use your own code.
- Use counts only from your friend's own device, not from one of yours.

## Troubleshooting

**"There is no such invite code."** Check the code; capital letters don't
matter.

**"Invite codes are for new accounts."** The friend's account is more than a
week old or isn't a trial.

**The bot can't see my friend in the channel.** They must join
[the channel](https://t.me/sstp_shield) first, then press **I joined**
again.

Anything else: [contact us](/contact).
