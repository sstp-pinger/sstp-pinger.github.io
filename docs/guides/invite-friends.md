# Invite friends

Share SSTP Shield with a friend and you both get free days:

| | Reward |
|---|---|
| **You** | **+7 days** on your access |
| **Your friend** | **+3 days** on their free trial |

There is no limit on how many friends you can invite. Each friend counts once.

## How to invite

1. In the app open **Unlock → Invite a friend** (or **Settings → Account →
   Invite a friend**).
2. Press **Share via Telegram** and pick a chat, or **Copy link** and send it
   anywhere. The link looks like `https://t.me/sstp_shield_bot?start=ref_…` and
   is yours for good.
3. The card shows how many friends you have invited and the days they earned
   you.

## What your friend does

1. Installs SSTP Shield and opens it once — the free trial starts by itself.
2. Opens your link. It starts our Telegram bot.
3. Joins the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/sstp_shield_softether) and presses
   **I joined**. The bot checks that they really are in it.
4. Sends the bot their **username** (in the app: **Settings → Account →
   Username**, with a copy button).

The bot then adds the days to both accounts. Your new end date shows in the app
the next time it refreshes the server list.

## Rules

- The friend must be **new**: an account from the free trial, started within
  the last week. Someone who already has a paid or older account can't be
  invited.
- A Telegram profile and an app account each count **once, ever**. You can't
  use your own link.
- If your friend **leaves the channel within a week**, both rewards are taken
  back. After a week they stay.

## Troubleshooting

**The bot says it can't see my friend in the channel.** They must join
[the channel](https://t.me/sstp_shield_softether) first, then press **I joined**
again.

**"No account with that username."** The friend needs to open the app once so
the trial starts, then copy the username from **Settings → Account**.

**"Not a fresh free trial."** The friend's account is more than a week old or
isn't a trial. An invite only works for new accounts.

Anything else: [contact us](/contact).
