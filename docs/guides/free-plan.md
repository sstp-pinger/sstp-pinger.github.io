# Free plan

After the free trial you can keep using SSTP Shield for free, with a smaller
set of servers. A subscription gives you everything.

| | Free plan | Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Servers | Your own set of 10 a day, picked for you | The whole tested list |
| Country choice | — | Yes |
| Auto-switch | Yes, with a short pause before each switch | Instant |
| **Try another server** | With a cooldown | Any time |
| Regional pool | — | Yes |
| Proxy sharing | — | Yes |
| Devices | One at a time | One phone and one computer |

## How to get it

When the trial ends, the app opens with **Free forever** at the top.

- **Join our channel.** Press **Join the channel in Telegram**. Our bot opens:
  join the [channel](https://t.me/sstp_shield_softether), press **I joined**,
  and come back to the app — it switches over by itself.
- **Use a friend's invite code.** Under **Have an invite code?**, type the code
  a friend sent you. This needs no Telegram at all.

**Telegram doesn't open without a VPN?** Once a day the app can connect you for
10 minutes so you can reach Telegram and join. The connection ends when the 10
minutes are up.

## Good to know

- Your 10 servers are **yours for the day**. If none of them work, press
  **New set** for another 10 — up to three times a day.
- **Leaving the channel pauses** a free plan you got through the channel.
  Join again and it comes back.
- One Telegram account can turn on the free plan for **one** SSTP Shield
  account.
- A subscription — [Telegram Stars or USDT](/docs/faq#how-do-i-pay) — or a
  [promo code](/docs/faq#do-you-have-promo-codes) adds full access on top.
